Document Q&A Agent approved

Upload a PDF, ask questions, get answers only from that document.

The Unique Kernel

Grounded answers only. The agent answers from its own knowledge base, the chunks of your uploaded PDF, so no assumptions or hallucinations. If the answer isn't in the document it says "not found in this document."

Who It's For

A student (mostly), or anyone holding a document of hundreds of pages: a syllabus, subject notes, a bill, a constitution. They need two or three things from it. Today they read page by page.

The Core Loop and the Proof

The "oh, that's cool" beat: ask something the document doesn't cover and get "not found in this document" instead of a guess.
